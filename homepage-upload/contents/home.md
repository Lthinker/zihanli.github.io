I am a PhD student in Biomedical Engineering at **Tsinghua University**, working at the Center for Biomedical Imaging Research, Tsinghua Medicine, advised by Professors **Hongen Liao**, **Qiyuan Tian**, and **Liangyi Chen**. I develop **generalizable AI for accessible medical imaging**, bringing advanced imaging methods closer to routine clinical practice and resource-limited settings.

My work spans **quantitative brain MRI** and **autonomous robotic ultrasound**. I develop self-supervised and physics-informed methods for fast diffusion MRI modeling and image denoising, and explore portable robotic systems that reduce dependence on expert ultrasound operators.

**Research interests:** Diffusion MRI & Microstructural Imaging · Self-supervised & Physics-informed Learning · Autonomous Ultrasound & Medical Robotics
